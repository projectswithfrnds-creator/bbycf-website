Our Mission
“There is no wellness industry without the teachers who teach.”

Body Bend Yoga Community Foundation invests in yoga teachers by providing scholarships for continuing education and professional development. Any active yoga teacher can apply for support toward teacher trainings, continuing education, advanced certifications, mentorships, and other approved educational opportunities.

For TeachersScholarships remove the barrier.

Active yoga teachers can request support for approved training, certifications, mentorships, and learning opportunities that deepen their practice.

For PartnersA shared network funds the work.

Studios donate space for fundraising events. Wellness brands sponsor and partner. Corporations hire teachers for wellness programs and make tax-deductible donations or sponsorships.

For CommunitiesTeacher growth creates a ripple.

Every financial gift, in-kind contribution, and partnership helps teachers keep learning so they can better serve the communities around them.

Nationwide network vision. California-based programs today.

The Ripple Model

The ecosystem is simple: support teachers, strengthen everyone.

BBYCF gathers support from studios, wellness brands, corporations, donors, and community partners, then turns that support into scholarship funding for teacher education.

Who We Are

A foundation for the teachers behind wellness.

BBYCF is building a nationwide network while currently serving communities through California-based programs. The work is focused on one clear belief: teacher education is community infrastructure.

What BBYCF Does

We turn community support into teacher scholarships.

BBYCF connects donors, studios, wellness brands, corporations, and community partners so active yoga teachers can access the education and mentorship they need to keep growing.

  • Any active yoga teacher can apply for scholarship support.
  • Approved funds can support trainings, continuing education, certifications, mentorships, and related learning opportunities.
  • Studios help by donating space for fundraising events.
  • Wellness brands and corporations help through sponsorships, partnerships, corporate wellness programs, and tax-deductible support.
  • Every contribution helps teachers better serve their communities.
Founder & President

Drew Muse

President (CEO)

Drew Muse began her yoga journey in 2010 to treat her scoliosis and reduce anxiety in school. Now a multi-certified yoga instructor and meditation guide, she hosts wellness events, classes, and teaches private sessions in Oakland and LA. She is also an instructor in various fitness apps and works as an ambassador for wellness brands, combining her love of wellness, community, and creativity all in one place.

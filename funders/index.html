How Our Funding Model Works

All figures, growth, and endowment assumptions for BBYCF funders and donors. For full donor-impact details and the latest scholarship goals, contact the board.

Funding Assumptions

Conservative growth, long-term teacher support.

These assumptions are referenced from the current projections model and should be reviewed with the board before any formal donor package is published.

Key Financial Assumptions

  • Year 1 to Year 2 Revenue Growth Rate: 20.0% (Conservative: 15 to 25%)
  • Year 2 to Year 3 Revenue Growth Rate: 25.0% (Growth phase: 20 to 30%)
  • Year 3 to Year 4 Revenue Growth Rate: 20.0%
  • Year 4 to Year 5 Revenue Growth Rate: 15.0% (Mature: 10 to 20%)
  • Expense Growth Rate vs Revenue: 80.0% (Expenses grow slower than revenue)

Endowment Model

  • Endowment Goal: $100,000
  • Annual Draw Rate: 5.0%
  • Investment Return Rate: 7.0%

Growth rates and endowment assumptions are referenced directly in the Projections sheet. For more detail, contact the BBYCF board.
